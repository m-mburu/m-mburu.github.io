Kenya Food Prices

Explore how food prices vary across time, counties, markets, commodities, and retail or wholesale price types in Kenya. The dashboard uses World Food Programme data published through the Humanitarian Data Exchange and lets you work in Kenyan shillings or US dollars. Use the overview, trend, map, comparison, and coverage views to identify recent price changes and differences between places.

Interactive dashboard

Kenya Indicators

Explore long-run changes in Kenya’s national DHS indicators across fertility, child survival, health services, nutrition, WASH, HIV, malaria, education, and agency. The dashboard connects curated household-survey evidence to SDG themes while keeping survey years, coverage, uncertainty, and data limitations visible. Use the overview to scan policy trends, inspect individual indicators in detail, or download the filtered catalogue for further analysis.

R package

ineqTrees

ineqTrees provides tools for studying socioeconomic inequality in health outcomes using tree-based models. It includes weighted-rank and concentration-index utilities together with methods for fitting inequality-aware trees and forests. The documentation covers model fitting, tuning, visualization, and SHAP-based decomposition through worked examples.
